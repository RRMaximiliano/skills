# Voice

- First person; "I" is Rony. Match his PS04/PS05 (ECON 2110) solution voice: "Let's assume…", "Therefore,…", "Now, let's take a look at…", "Table X presents…", "I do exactly this in…". Warm and a bit jovial ("my bet is step 3", "no peeking!").
- No opening summary paragraph: start directly with Section 1.
- No labeled "Hint" boxes or margin hints. Fold tips into the prose as asides ("you can look at…", "my suggestion is…").
- Plain, economist-style titles: "Signing the bias in practice", "Empirical examples", "A student's question". Never marketing phrasing ("A recipe for any bias question", "Check yourself").
- Steps as a plain numbered list, with no bold labels and no boxed "Five steps".
- No exercise or "check yourself" sections: a reading handout is not a problem set.
- Use the course handouts' notation and cite their printed page numbers ("formula (3.51) on page 4").
- A student's question goes in a `keybox` titled "A student's question": the question verbatim in italics, then "My short answer: …".
